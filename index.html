What makes SSPU's B.Sc. in Beauty and Wellness unique?

It is India's only Science Degree in Beauty & Wellness, offered by SSPU — India's #1 NIRF-ranked Skill University (2024 & 2025). The program combines a 70% practical learning model, 100+ industry MOUs, and collaborative brand tie-ups with Enrich Salon, VLCC, Lakmé Salon, Tira, Kaashi Wellness, Lee's Spa, and more — ensuring graduates are industry-ready from day one.

India's Only Science Degree 70% Practical 100+ Industry MOUs

What is the CIDESCO Aesthetics++ Diploma?

It is a part-time diploma offered alongside the degree. Eligibility is a minimum 12th pass with at least 3 years of relevant industry experience. The duration is 300 hours over 3 months, subject to fulfilment of minimum required seats.

Part Time 300 Hours / 3 Months 12th Pass + 3 Yrs Experience

Which companies recruit SSPU Beauty & Wellness graduates?

Yearly internships and placements are offered with top companies, backed by 100+ MOUs with global beauty, wellness, and hospitality industries. Partners include The Taj Mahal Palace, Enrich Salon, VLCC, Kaashi Wellness, Lakmé Salon, Tira and Lee's Spa.

VLCC Lakmé Salon The Taj Mahal Palace Kaashi Wellness

What campus facilities are available for students?

The Pune campus houses class-apart practical training labs for skin care, hair, makeup, spa therapy, and cosmetic science — including a Skin & Aesthetics Treatment Lab, a Spa & Wellness Therapy Studio and an Innovation & Start-up Center.

Practical Training Labs Skin & Aesthetics Lab Spa & Wellness Studio Start-up Center

Are there global exposure opportunities within the degree?

Yes. SSPU has 9+ global university partners across the USA, Germany, France, Australia and Turkey, and the degree is internationally recognised — designed to match global industry standards for the international beauty, wellness and fashion aesthetics industry.

9+ University Partners USA & Germany France, Australia & Turkey
